## 🗣️ BOSTON SAYS — Opinion Poll Layer

> Not prediction markets. Real-time sentiment questions that run alongside markets and can auto-generate predictions.

### Seed Polls

| Poll | Options |
|------|---------|
| Best first-date restaurant right now? | Giulia · Pammy's · Little Donkey · Bar Mezzana · Sarma |
| Most overrated Boston restaurant? | Grill 23 · Legal Sea Foods · Toro · Menton · Marliave |
| Which neighborhood feels like it's about to blow up? | East Boston · Dorchester · Allston · Hyde Park · Roslindale |
| Best new bar in Boston? | Alcove · Lolita Fort Point · Yvonne's · Bijou · Silvertone |
| Which restaurant is impossible to get into but worth it? | Tasting Counter · Giulia · O Ya · Sarma · Row 34 |
| What's the next food trend to hit Boston? | Korean BBQ · Smash burgers · Natural wine bars · Omakase · Filipino |
| Which Boston neighborhood will have the most new restaurant openings in 2027? | East Boston · South End · Seaport · JP/Rozzie · Dorchester |

### Mechanics
1. Users vote (anonymous or logged-in)
2. Results tally in real-time on the Boston Says card
3. Leading option can auto-promote → prediction market
4. E.g. "East Boston 38% → AHEAD auto-creates: *Will East Boston see the greatest restaurant growth among these neighborhoods over 12 months?*"
