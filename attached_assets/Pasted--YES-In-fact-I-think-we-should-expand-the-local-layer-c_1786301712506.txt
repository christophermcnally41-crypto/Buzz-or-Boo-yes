**YES.** In fact, I think we should expand the **local layer considerably**. What you're describing could be one of the things that makes the platform feel alive rather than like a generic forecasting site.

The key is turning *“What's the hottest restaurant in Boston?”* into something objectively measurable.

For example:

> **Which restaurant will gain more Google reviews over the next 30 days?**
> Restaurant A — 61%
> Restaurant B — 39%

Or:

> **Will [Restaurant] reach a 4.6★ Google rating by October 1?**
> YES — 72%
> NO — 28%

Those can actually resolve.

## 🍸 I'd create an entire LOCAL PULSE category

Not just Boston weather and housing.

Think:

**Eat · Drink · Shop · Openings · Neighborhoods · Nightlife · Real Estate · Events**

And then every city eventually gets its own prediction universe.

### Restaurants

These could be fantastic:

> **Which restaurant will gain more Google reviews this month?**
> Restaurant A vs. Restaurant B

> **Will [new restaurant] maintain 4.5★+ on Google after its first 250 reviews?**

> **Will [restaurant] reach 1,000 Google reviews before December 31?**

> **Will [restaurant]'s Google rating rise from 4.4★ to 4.5★ by November?**

> **Which new Boston restaurant will have the highest Google rating six months after opening?**

> **Will [restaurant] remain above 4.6★ through year-end?**

> **Which restaurant gains more Instagram followers during September?**

> **Will reservations at [hot restaurant] still be unavailable for prime Saturday-night seating two months from now?**

That last one would need a carefully defined reservation source and exact measurement procedure, but it's doable.

# I think our original 71 should actually change

Rather than having 25 Boston questions dominated by weather/real estate, I'd break the product more like:

**20 — Local Pulse**
Restaurants, bars, retail, openings, neighborhoods, events

**15 — Local Real Estate**
Individual properties + neighborhood trends

**10 — Local Weather**

**13 — Home & Interiors**

**13 — Fashion & Style**

= **71 launch markets**

That makes the app's identity much stronger.

Because there's already plenty of weather prediction.

But:

> **Which Boston restaurant will be hotter in 60 days?**

> **Will this new South End restaurant maintain 4.6 stars?**

> **Will this Back Bay condo sell above asking?**

> **Will chrome beat brass?**
