# VERSION 3: Real-money markets

Eventually your screens could have actual:

**YES $0.63**

**NO $0.37**

and people trade real contracts.

But **this is where the regulatory situation changes dramatically.**

The CFTC currently describes prediction markets as markets for event-contract derivatives. A prediction market listing these contracts has to comply with the regulatory framework governing derivatives markets, including rules intended to prevent contracts from being readily susceptible to manipulation. ([Commodity Futures Trading Commission][1])

And this area is moving *right now*. In 2026 the CFTC has issued new prediction-market advisories and is actively pursuing rulemaking concerning event contracts. ([Commodity Futures Trading Commission][2])

So I would **not** recommend attempting to personally create an unlicensed real-money prediction exchange.

Instead:

## Build the product.

Then partner with the regulated financial infrastructure.

That's potentially much more achievable.

There are considerably more CFTC-designated contract markets now than there were just a few years ago; the CFTC's current list shows multiple new DCM designations during June and July 2026 alone. ([Commodity Futures Trading Commission][3])

# The hardest part isn't actually building the app

It's:

## **How do markets resolve?**

Every question needs a definitive answer.

Take:

> Will chocolate brown be the biggest interior color of 2027?

Terrible prediction-market wording.

Who's deciding “biggest”?

Instead:

> Will Google search interest for “chocolate brown interior” exceed “sage green interior” during October–December 2027?

Now we have data.

Or:

> Will more than 15% of Architectural Digest's featured living rooms during Q4 contain predominantly brown upholstery?

Potentially measurable—but you'd need a clearly defined methodology and content/data rights.

Or:

> Will Pantone select a blue-family color as 2028 Color of the Year?

Easy.

The market rules identify Pantone's announcement as the resolution source.

Your platform needs what prediction-market people often call an **oracle** or resolution mechanism:

### Question

What exactly are we predicting?

### Deadline

When does the market close?

### Resolution source

Who determines reality?

### Criteria

What exactly constitutes YES?

### Dispute procedure

What happens if the data is unclear?

This would be one of the company's most important systems.
